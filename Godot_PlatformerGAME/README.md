## Assignment extension

This project targets Godot 4.7.2 and retains the original starter level while adding two connected playable scenes: `Scenes/level_two.tscn` (Neon Relay) and `Scenes/level_three.tscn` (Sky Hop).

- The original level exit opens only after its five collectibles are collected and advances to Neon Relay.
- Neon Relay has six collectibles, rotating hazards that reset the player, a locked exit, and a completion screen.
- Sky Hop is a short, easy jumping course with three collectibles and a final exit.
- The supplied low-poly Soldier FBX is used as the visible player character. A small procedural idle/run/jump motion layer keeps it in sync with the starter controller while preserving the original controls (WASD and Space).
